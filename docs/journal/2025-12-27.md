# 2025-12-27

Finally after all the changes, we were able to get a full training run (50 epochs) on Google Colab

The best `val_loss` we got was `4.2015  ppl 67` which is not the best in any way but it’s definitely progress

One thing to notice is that we reach the gradient minima around epoch 20, it just goes down and up a bit but mostly stays around that 4.2-2.7 mark

The only thing that can help us is making the model deeper now

Since we’re working with 80 GB VRAM, gradient accumulation will help here, by halfing our microbatch size

### Next steps

- Architectural changes
    - Increase layers to 24 (from 12), deeper representations
    - Use GeLU instead of ReLU for better activations as it is also standard for GPT-2 based architectures
        - GELU is smoother and helps the optimizer navigate the complex landscape of a 24-layer model much better than the sharp corners of ReLU
    - Use GPT-2 based initialisations as with 24 layers, we run a high risk of gradient explosion with standard (random) initialisation
        - Scale the weights of residual projections by $`1/\sqrt{2 \times N_{layers}}`$. This keeps the signal variance controlled as it propagates up the deep network.
